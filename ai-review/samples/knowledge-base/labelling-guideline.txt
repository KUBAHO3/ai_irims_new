Guidelines on labelling of medicinal products

1. Storage conditions
The outer and immediate packaging must state the storage conditions, including a specific temperature or temperature range, for example "Store below 30°C". Vague statements such as "store in a cool, dry place" are not acceptable on their own.

2. Expiry date
The label must show the expiry date in the format MM/YYYY, preceded by "EXP".

3. Product identification
The label must show the product name, the strength and the pharmaceutical form, and the name of the manufacturer.

4. Language
Labels must be in English, French or Kinyarwanda.
