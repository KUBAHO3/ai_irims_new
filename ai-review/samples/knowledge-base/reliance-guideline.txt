Guidelines on the reliance (abridged) registration pathway for human medicines

1. Declaration of sameness
The applicant must submit a declaration of sameness, signed by the marketing authorization holder, confirming that the product submitted to Rwanda FDA is the same as the product approved by the reference regulatory authority in formulation, manufacturing site, manufacturing process, specifications and packaging.

2. GMP certificate
A Good Manufacturing Practice certificate issued by the reference regulatory authority must be submitted for every manufacturing site. The certificate must be valid at the time of submission.

3. Letter of authorization
The applicant must submit a letter authorizing the reference regulatory authority to share assessment and inspection reports about the product with Rwanda FDA.
