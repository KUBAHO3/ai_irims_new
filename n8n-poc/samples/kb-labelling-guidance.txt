Section 3. Labelling of medicinal products

3.1 Storage conditions
The outer and immediate label must state the storage conditions. The statement must include
a temperature limit or range, for example "Store below 30°C" or "Store between 2°C and 8°C".
General phrases such as "store properly" or "store in a cool place" without a temperature
are not acceptable.

3.2 Shelf life and expiry
The label must state the expiry date. The shelf life approved in the registration dossier
must be consistent with the expiry date format shown on the proposed label.

Section 4. Manufacturer information

4.1 The dossier must state the name and full physical address of each manufacturing site.
4.2 A Good Manufacturing Practice (GMP) certificate issued by a competent authority must be
provided for each site. Certificates must be valid on the date the application is submitted.

Section 5. Quality control

5.1 At least one certificate of analysis for a production batch of the finished product
must be provided. It must show the batch number, specifications and test results.
